# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Overworld becomes open ocean with islands, archipelagos and rare large islands; terrain and biomes stay vanilla
  (or modded).
- Default, Large Biomes and Amplified world types; Tectonic support.
- Ocean monuments about six times sparser.
- All changes ship as the built-in `open_seas` data pack, on by default and switchable per world.
